Colour

White, one electric blue, cool greys, and one red that only ever means removed, dead or negative. The values shown are read from this page as it is drawn, so they change with the theme.

Type

Four fonts, each with one job. Reading text is Geist; anything that is a label, a number or a control is one of the other three.

Geist · --font-textFear of the TalibanReading text, titles of things, sentences. Weights 400, 500, 560, 600, 620.
Geist Mono · --font-mono15 May 2026 · v6.0Buttons, dates, meta lines, the footer credit. Weights 400, 500.
Geist Pixel (Square) · --font-pixel47 COUNTRIESTags, eyebrows, numerals, small capital labels, the first word of the wordmark.
Geist Pixel Line · --font-displayCOUNTRY NOTESThe page title only: one per page, blue, uppercase.

Lines, corners, shadow, time

Hairlines hold the page together. Corners are square. A shadow means “this floats above the page” and nothing else. Three durations and one easing curve move everything.

Buttons and links

One filled blue button for the main thing to do; the rest are outlined or quiet. Labels are plain verbs in mono.

Tags, chips, badges

Say it with a tag, not a paragraph. A tag names the kind of thing; a chip opens a little more; a badge is a quiet status.

Controls

Square, and they glide: the thumb slides, the switch slides, the tick grows. All of these work.

Search, results, fields

One search box. Results come in groups, each under a heading with its count; a row is a line of text on a hairline, not a box.

Cards, frames, callouts

A card is a hairline box with no shadow and no rounding. Its top line is tags and a date; then the title; then one line of meta; then the buttons.

Tooltips, popovers, menus, toasts

These are the only things with a shadow. Each eases in from where it was called (a small rise and a de-blur) and eases out again.

Tables

A table sits in a hairline box that scrolls sideways on its own, so it never widens the page. Figures line up (tabular numerals).

Empty, loading, error

Honest and plain: say what is the case, then offer the next step. Loading never spins.

The dotted motif

The globe is dots, so the pictures are dots: the product mark, the flags, the markers. Dots are also the things you click, with an invisible target larger than the dot. Rest the pointer on a flag below.

Motion

No snaps. Things arrive by easing in, leave by easing out, change by crossfading, and open by gliding. Nothing around them moves.

Layout

On a desktop the app is a shell: header, content that scrolls inside, footer always in view. On a phone it is an ordinary page that scrolls, with the footer at its end.
