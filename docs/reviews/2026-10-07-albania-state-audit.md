# Albanian reimbursement implementation evidence, 7 October 2026

Two original KLSH state audits add limited implementation evidence to the
existing MiCLU discussion of paragraph 7.1.2. The September 2019 report's page 111
records spending under the chronic reimbursement category and limits in tracking
beneficiaries. The May 2024 report's page 23 says diagnosis-level prescription
data were not supplied despite requests; page 117 recommends checking eligibility.
Neither establishes free medicine for every mental-health condition or patient.
